CorpoSuites ERP Development Roadmap After HRM

Recommended Module Development Order After HRM:

1. WORKFLOW ENGINE (Foundation Module)

Purpose: Create a common approval and automation layer before adding
more enterprise modules.

Features: - Approval Levels - Single, Sequential and Parallel
Approvals - Approval Templates - Workflow Builder - Custom Status
Management - Approval History - Rejection and Resubmission Flow -
Notification Trigger System - Module-wise Workflow Configuration

2. CRM MODULE

Purpose: Manage customer acquisition, sales pipeline and relationship
management.

Features: - CRM Dashboard - Lead Sources - Lead Statuses - Leads
Management - Contacts Management - Companies Management - Customer
Relationship History - Opportunities - Sales Pipeline Kanban -
Activities (Call, Meeting, Email) - Follow Ups and Reminders - Email
Communication History - Quotations - Sales Forecasting - CRM Reports

3. SALES MODULE

Purpose: Convert opportunities into revenue and manage complete sales
lifecycle.

Features: - Customers - Customer Groups - Customer Credit Limit -
Payment Terms - Price Lists - Sales Quotations - Sales Orders - Delivery
Management - Delivery Notes - Sales Invoices - Credit Notes - Sales
Returns - POS System - Sales Targets - Sales Commission - Sales Reports

4. PURCHASE MODULE

Purpose: Manage supplier relationships and procurement process.

Features: - Vendors - Vendor Groups - Vendor Performance - Purchase
Requests - Approval Workflow - RFQ Management - Supplier Quotations -
Purchase Orders - Goods Receipts - Purchase Invoice Matching - Debit
Notes - Purchase Returns - Purchase Reports

5. INVENTORY MODULE

Purpose: Control stock movement and warehouse operations.

Features: - Warehouses - Warehouse Locations - Stock Entry - Opening
Stock - Stock Adjustment - Stock Transfer - Stock Count - Batch
Management - Serial Number Management - Inventory Transactions - Stock
Valuation - Reorder Level - Low Stock Alerts - Inventory Reports

6. PRODUCT MANAGEMENT MODULE

Purpose: Create a complete product master system connected with sales,
purchase and inventory.

Features: - Categories - Sub Categories - Brands - Units - Unit
Conversion - Product Attributes - Attribute Values - Product Variants -
Products - Product Images - Product Bundles - Pricing Management -
Discount Rules - Barcode Generator - Product Reports

7. ACCOUNTING MODULE

Purpose: Manage complete financial operations and reporting.

Features: - Chart of Accounts - Account Types - Journal Entries -
General Ledger - Cash Book - Bank Accounts - Bank Transactions - Bank
Reconciliation - Accounts Receivable - Accounts Payable - Payment
Receive - Payment Make - Trial Balance - Profit and Loss - Balance
Sheet - Cash Flow - Tax Management - Financial Reports

8. ASSET MANAGEMENT MODULE

Purpose: Manage company assets and their lifecycle.

Features: - Asset Categories - Asset Register - Asset Purchase
Information - Asset Assignment - Employee Asset Tracking - Asset
Location Tracking - Maintenance Schedule - Maintenance History -
Depreciation Calculation - Disposal Management - Asset Reports

9. PROJECT MANAGEMENT MODULE

Purpose: Manage client projects, resources and profitability.

Features: - Clients - Projects - Project Budgets - Project Teams -
Milestones - Tasks - Task Board Kanban - Gantt Chart - Task
Dependencies - Time Tracking - Timesheets - Project Expenses - Project
Billing - Project Profitability Reports

10. DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT MODULE

Purpose: Centralize company documents and approvals.

Features: - Document Categories - Document Storage - Document Search -
Metadata Management - Contracts - Contract Expiry Reminder - Digital
Signature - Version Control - Document Access Permission - Document
Audit Trail

11. SUPPORT MODULE

Purpose: Manage customer and internal support operations.

Features: - Ticket Categories - Ticket Creation - Ticket Assignment -
Ticket Status - Priority Management - SLA Management - Escalation
Rules - Knowledge Base - Customer Portal - Support Reports

12. BUDGET & FINANCE MODULE

Purpose: Control planning, spending and business performance.

Features: - Budget Planning - Department Budget - Project Budget -
Budget Allocation - Actual vs Budget Comparison - Cost Centers - Profit
Centers - Financial Forecasting - Budget Reports

13. REPORTS & EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD

Purpose: Provide complete business intelligence.

Features: - Executive Dashboard - Sales Analytics - Purchase Analytics -
Inventory Analytics - Accounting Reports - HR Reports - Customer
Reports - Vendor Reports - Custom Report Builder - Export PDF/Excel -
Scheduled Reports

FINAL ERP BUILD ORDER

1.  HRM (Completed)
2.  Workflow Engine
3.  CRM
4.  Sales
5.  Purchase
6.  Inventory
7.  Product Management
8.  Accounting
9.  Asset Management
10. Project Management
11. Document Management
12. Support
13. Budget & Finance
14. Reports & Executive Dashboard

Architecture Dependency Reason: - Workflow Engine should come early
because Sales, Purchase, Accounting, HRM and Support require
approvals. - CRM should come before Sales because opportunities convert
into sales documents. - Product Management should be completed before
Sales, Purchase and Inventory because all transactions depend on
products. - Inventory should be connected with Sales and Purchase before
Accounting integration. - Accounting should be implemented after
transaction modules because it receives automated journal entries. -
Reports should be developed last because all modules generate reporting
data.
